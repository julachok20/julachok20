## Featured Projects

### 🌱 NongRak AI (น้องรัก)
A Thai childcare app for parents of young children. It tracks feeding, sleep, growth, medicines and vaccinations, and answers questions in Thai from the family's own records. Parents control exactly what co-parents, grandparents and nannies can see, and LINE reminders keep care on schedule. The AI states facts only, never medical conclusions, and anything resembling an emergency routes to 1669 first.
`Kotlin` · `Jetpack Compose` · `Supabase` · `AI (Gemini / Claude)` · `LINE API`

### ✨ [Mira](https://github.com/julachok20/mira)
A Thai investing app for first-job earners, built for learning with simulated money. Two pooled paper portfolios (Safe and Growth) are updated every market day by a Python model, with fair unit-based accounting. It is research and education only, not investment advice.
`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `Python` · `Supabase` · `Clerk`
